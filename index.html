Embedding the Verified by ProvenVerdicts badge

ProvenVerdicts is an independent directory of personal injury attorneys and law firms across the United States. Every verdict and settlement it lists is documented from court records, legal press or the firm's own filings before it is published. A firm with verified results can show that on its own site with a badge that links to its ProvenVerdicts page, where visitors can read each result and its sources.

What the badge looks like

Light, for white and pale backgrounds.

Dark, for navy and black backgrounds (?theme=dark).

The badge is an SVG served by ProvenVerdicts, so the count on it updates by itself as new results are verified.

The snippet

Replace your-firm with your firm's slug, the last part of your ProvenVerdicts address (provenverdicts.com/your-firm). Your badge page, provenverdicts.com/your-firm/badge, has this code pre-filled with a copy button.

For a dark background, add ?theme=dark to the image address.

One badge per result

Each verified result also has its own badge, linking straight to that result's write-up. The image address is https://provenverdicts.com/badge/your-firm/result-slug.svg, and the link goes to the result page. Your badge page lists every one of yours with the code ready to copy.

Rules

  • Use the badge as served. Do not edit the image, change its colours or recreate it.
  • Keep the link to your ProvenVerdicts page on the badge. The badge is a claim; the link is the proof.
  • Keep the one-line note under it. State bar advertising rules in most states require it next to any mention of past results.
  • Only a firm or attorney with verified results on ProvenVerdicts may show the badge. It cannot be bought, and it is removed when a listing's results are withdrawn.

Getting yours

Find your firm on provenverdicts.com and open its badge page, or read how listings work for law firms. How results are verified is set out in the methodology. If a result of yours is missing, submit it there with its documentation and the editorial team reviews it.
